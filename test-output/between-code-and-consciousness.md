## Prologue: The View from Above


At age 18, just before starting my career at Tecnológico de Monterrey, I experienced a brain tumor crisis that profoundly shaped my worldview. During my high school graduation ceremony, I suffered a severe seizure and cerebral hemorrhage, leading to the discovery of a brain abscess requiring emergency surgery.



The experience was transformative. I described an out-of-body, almost mystical experience during recovery where I felt myself floating above my body, observing from the ceiling. This near-death experience led to deep introspection about life, mortality, and purpose, fundamentally changing my approach to relationships, ambition, and spirituality.


## Part I: Early Life and Foundation


### Personal Background and Family


I was born in San Pedro Garza García, Nuevo León, Mexico in July 1991. My father is Mohammad Reza Azarang Esfandiari, PhD, a highly accomplished academic and industrial engineer who has worked at Tecnológico de Monterrey since 1986 as a professor in the Industrial Engineering department.



My father has an impressive international academic profile: PhD in Administrative Engineering from City University of London, England (1998), Master's in Administration from Tecnológico de Monterrey (1992), Master's in Industrial Engineering from Queen's University of Belfast, Northern Ireland (1981), Bachelor's in Electronic Engineering with Honors from Oxford Brookes University, England (1980), and Specialization in Quality Systems and Manufacturing from JUSE Institute, Tokyo, Japan. He served as Former Director of the Quality Center at ITESM and worked in the Mexican Presidency (2003) as Chief of Office for Strategic Planning.



Rashid attributes his core values to his family, particularly emphasizing his mother's "honest love that isn't always pretty like in movies" and his father's strength, determination, and emotional/physical health. His father, described as a marathon runner, instilled the philosophy of "walk, keep walking, and don't look back" when facing life's challenges.


## Part II: Professional Journey and Entrepreneurial Evolution


### Early Entrepreneurship (2012-2014)


At 17, I began my entrepreneurial journey with several failed attempts. My most notable early venture was "Mint - Multiple Interactions", a disruptive technology using Japanese infrared sensor technology from Nintendo Wii to create a wireless camera that could transform any glass surface into a touchscreen interface, capable of detecting 48 simultaneous touchpoints for only $40.



Despite assembling a team of 16 people including 6 engineering students from Tecnológico de Monterrey, the venture failed in 2013 due to my inexperience in finance, administration, and business strategy.


### Digital Agency and International Expansion (2014-2016)


Following the failure, I pivoted to founding Esfandiari, a digital agency connecting freelancers globally to provide technology, marketing, and design services. I later partnered with my father and formed a joint venture with Loma Marketing (based in Los Angeles, Austin, and San Francisco), focusing on digital media investment for online sales across 25+ countries in 4 continents.


### Leadership and Scale (2017-2022)


**Head of Technology at Grupo Sunmall (2017-2019)**: At age 26, I became CTO of a major real estate group under businessman Jorge Sada-Solano, implementing SaaS tools and technology integration for shopping center construction projects.



**Sales Development Manager (2019-2020)**: Built and managed a remote team of 60+ employees across Latin America to support US startups with sales and marketing development.



**Project Leader at Pronalab México (2020-2022)**: Developed a comprehensive COVID-19 testing platform that processed over 100,000 tests per month and served 1M+ users, with 200+ operators working in the system.


### Recent Ventures and Innovation (2023-2025)


**Head of Product at Nulearn.ai (2023)**: Focused on improving educational experiences for teenagers.



I created **AyudaLocal.com**, a platform enhancing local business visibility and fostering community trust, and developed **Affihub.com**, a comprehensive platform for advanced communication and service management in the remote work era. However, I've since stopped my entrepreneurship of Affihub and AyudaLocal to go all in with AI, focusing on building data integrations, data warehouses and software architecture work. On the side, I'm doing research on AI, Cognitive Infrastructure and Epistemic Physics.
