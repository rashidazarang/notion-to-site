## Key Features


![image](notion:ff458532-8d46-48c3-983a-68a4b087db45)


**Effortless service provider discovery:** 


AyudaLocal’s extensive selection and search criteria make it easy for customers to find the ideal professional for their specific needs.


**Transparent pricing and fees:**


AyudaLocal clearly displays service prices, taxes, and fees, empowering customers to make informed decisions before hiring a service provider.


**Robust review system:**


AyudaLocal maintains trust and quality within its platform through a comprehensive review system for both customers and service providers, fostering informed decision-making and promoting high-quality work.


**Secure and versatile payment options:**


AyudaLocal offers multiple payment methods, including cash and online payments through Stripe, providing customers with the convenience and security they need when making transactions.


**Flexible cancellation policy:**


AyudaLocal's customer-centric cancellation policy allows customers to cancel job requests within a specified timeframe without incurring charges.


**Real-time communication between parties:**


AyudaLocal's platform facilitates seamless communication between customers and service providers, streamlining negotiations, scheduling, and any required adjustments or clarifications.
