# **Prompt for Generating the Marketing Copy for a Persuasive Sales Website** 


## What does it do?


This prompt instructs the AI to generate marketing copy based on a detailed and structured framework. Here's a breakdown of its function:

1. **Guidance on Framework Use:** It explicitly directs the AI to employ a specific framework for creating marketing copy. This framework includes elements such as engaging headlines, personal testimonials, scientific backing, visual proof, problem-solution dynamics, endorsements, benefits listing, social proof, urgency, clear calls to action, risk reversal, and maintaining authenticity.
2. **Customization According to Specifics:** The prompt requires the AI to tailor the copy based on particular details provided, such as topic, language, industry, market segment, product niche, marketing angle, tone and style, and any special instructions. This ensures that the content is highly relevant and targeted.
3. **Focus on Effective Marketing Elements:** By following the outlined framework, the AI's response is structured to touch upon key aspects that make marketing copy effective, especially in the context of dropshipping and affiliate marketing funnels. These elements are designed to engage, persuade, and convert readers into customers.
4. **Encouragement of Narrative and Emotional Engagement:** The inclusion of personal stories and testimonials, along with the emphasis on relatable problems and solutions, guides the AI to create content that emotionally resonates with the audience.
5. **Balancing Credibility and Persuasion:** The prompt balances the emotional aspects of marketing with the need for credibility and authenticity, guiding the AI to include scientific backing, expert opinions, and genuine testimonials.
6. **Conversion Optimization:** Elements like a clear call to action, social proof, and creating a sense of urgency are geared towards optimizing the copy for higher conversion rates.
7. **Flexibility and Adaptability:** The prompt is structured to be adaptable for various products, services, and industries, demonstrating its versatility.

---


# **Prompt:**


"Hello AI, I need your assistance in creating marketing copy for a specific product or service. Please use the following framework to ensure the content is compelling and persuasive, especially for dropshipping and affiliate marketing funnels:


### **Framework for Effective Copy:**

1. **Engaging Headline:** Begin with a captivating headline to grab attention.
2. **Personal Testimonials and Stories:** Incorporate real-life stories and testimonials that resonate with the audience.
3. **Scientific Backing and Expert Opinion:** Include scientific evidence or expert opinions for credibility.
4. **Visual Proof:** Add before-and-after images or videos if available.
5. **Relatable Problems and Solutions:** Address common problems faced by the target audience and present the product as a solution.
6. **Celebrities or Influencer Endorsements:** Use endorsements from well-known figures if applicable.
7. **Simple and Clear Benefits:** Clearly list the benefits of the product or service.
8. **Social Proof:** Include user comments or testimonials for trust-building.
9. **Sense of Urgency:** Create urgency with limited-time offers.
10. **Clear Call to Action:** End with a strong call to action.
11. **Risk Reversal:** Mention any risk-free trials or guarantees.
12. **Stay Authentic:** Ensure authenticity and verifiability in the content.

Now, using this framework, please create marketing copy based on the following specifics:

- **Topic:** [Your Topic Here]
- **Language:** [Your Language Preference Here]
- **Industry:** [Your Industry Here]
- **Market Segment:** [Your Target Market Segment Here]
- **Product Niche:** [Your Product Niche Here]
- **Marketing Angle:** [Your Chosen Marketing Angle Here]
- **Tone and Style:** [Preferred Tone and Style of the Copy]
- **Special Instructions:** [Any Additional Instructions or Elements to Include]

_For example, if the topic is 'Eco-Friendly Yoga Mats', the language is English, industry is Fitness and Wellness, target market is yoga enthusiasts, the product niche is biodegradable yoga mats, the marketing angle is sustainability, tone is inspiring and upbeat, and special instructions include highlighting eco-friendly manufacturing processes._


Please create the marketing copy accordingly."
