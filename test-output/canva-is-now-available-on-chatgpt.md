# [**Canva**](/834ecd6208344724a548a8ff193a8fc6) is now available on ChatGPT


This update makes graphic design 10x easier. Checkout the guide were I’ll describe to ChatGPT what we want to create in Canva.


![image](notion:1f21c2c1-2a8e-4690-9c12-d3b53c81626d)


[OpenAI](https://link.mail.beehiiv.com/ss/c/YN19BvD2XAN6lk4nf0a0hynZCkA_293fVpv6mb-eUGnTAEcRt5vXKQ9YSbjIK840Ep06nSeoCgLJ0Csf0K0gbCT7gXwOH3FMraOoZRfGXxyt7ohvSRpwJWc92pHYSfOT5ugTtVsErD_prgFwEshIPQ/3zd/zkcA21tYQUeXnr_UwXw7CQ/h13/fQfjdgjdViDofZCMJUyulE4g63HahCIBZvbmoaqp2oY) has integrated [Canva](https://link.mail.beehiiv.com/ss/c/qgaYIN57snqIpEKqECZANMwPeR5y2D5pNwkkIgWVSkK9FeyPk0pa0CJyZzXWQCNag1KA-Mzqewr7dKRulKc9MS8UEwlzYXrJXQ818_CoVVq_6dMPs4SZetPo6UaOn33MeVlWdULOEU2PqcByuah0Aw/3zd/zkcA21tYQUeXnr_UwXw7CQ/h14/g8mdvj-e70fK-Tle6NhOd_86sxXtiBlia3hOVkDSEYM) through ChatGPT’s plugin platform to simplify the creation of digital visuals such as logos and banners. Users can access this feature by [simply installing](https://link.mail.beehiiv.com/ss/c/qgaYIN57snqIpEKqECZANKThLdVlKTAUcu0On9t3J2RRRfSOg9kWMBJjBxbm8fSZus13omabp9GWCbkLJsMdx_9HmUFwtSgZriDxfuL-u7fQWYSvOdttAIC9nChmSEWyZksZBESzRtKvafW-M6F3zx4J-QXIoceUsPLyEg3-RrDkcW-m0g3b-ZekTBN_769-/3zd/zkcA21tYQUeXnr_UwXw7CQ/h15/P7jXE0iunMOrW68hT6pSXIlADFOuKnzHKY3vRLYvF-c) the Canva plugin on ChatGPT’s interface. Once installed, users describe the visual they wish to create in the prompt box, generating a list of visual options that can be edited in Canva and downloaded.


The Canva plugin is accessible only to those on the ChatGPT Plus subscription plan. For a monthly fee of $20, subscribers gain the ability to create digital visuals effortlessly and benefit from the advanced capabilities of the GPT-4 language model, such as generating long-form content and image recognition.


OpenAI’s decision to integrate Canva is part of a broader strategy to enhance ChatGPT’s capabilities amid growing competition from other advanced models like [Claude AI](https://link.mail.beehiiv.com/ss/c/wiEhD9KpQ_vCRgaOPrqkjTy10QJGoUoeuOkviSka_a7U8m0G9Vv6PIi9zVqbydP59zLnJgghHEGpgDhikiGbWWj2ahKsqlY9KgY9ioEKyJuPgUSdJgA_Eo7Fz7zJvwawlwpTckq5uiPi1AjQ4Z6cgg/3zd/zkcA21tYQUeXnr_UwXw7CQ/h16/lCtVqqZF_tyxs87hjqZVjiwEnOdZ6s0_LQo2paGRsDY) and Google's [Bard](https://link.mail.beehiiv.com/ss/c/1I3_F9wayzJ8BWTNWKpKgkt-H0KcNvQBrpnn-6ZFeehTraGGqm2elbcv2VMO01AFW8KMe8AUW2zj7tNli_2ToLYnDPT_HLb35yRieUs_rLZHEdVhmCDTiCdYA5GHbl9clWV__y0pARyXD4FYQDgT6bzeiQC49rFoi_crVfY_DvE/3zd/zkcA21tYQUeXnr_UwXw7CQ/h17/KVZDHadgDS2jaers6nfeYYNCA4J3yavk8Q35dvQgvs4). Previously, OpenAI offered a web-browsing feature through Microsoft Bing, but this was removed due to issues that arose from its ability to access paywalled content.


The Canva integration is a significant value addition, especially for ChatGPT Plus subscribers. By streamlining the digital design process, this new functionality could attract a broader user base, catering to individuals and businesses looking for efficient digital design solutions.
