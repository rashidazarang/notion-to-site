# Get Meeting Attendance using Link Analytics


![image](notion:16252116-a8b1-80a5-800d-d9496d5a5b57)


## Get Meeting Attendance with Link Analytics


Tracking key meeting metrics like punctuality and attendance is a big deal. It’s how you know your sales process is working. But most businesses don’t have the tools to do it properly. The result? Missed opportunities, inefficiencies, and an incomplete picture of what’s working and what’s not.


That’s why we built a Link Management System, that has proven to be a simple yet powerful way to track and analyze meetings. Turning data into actionable insights so you can make smarter, more informed decisions.


---


## **Getting Data from the Meeting URL**


Here’s how it works: when you schedule a meeting, you start with a single Google Meet link.


![image](notion:16252116-a8b1-80f6-96fb-ea4652a5cf6c)


### **Custom Links with Built-In Analytics**


Our system creates two custom links that point to the same meeting URL. Each link is tailored to track specific behaviors. For the prospect we track attendance and for the sales team, we track punctuality. 


![image](notion:16252116-a8b1-80ee-9cb3-e9cc3aa6f129)


### **Real-Time Monitoring in Your CRM**


Key metrics are stored and visualized in real time within your CRM, providing instant insights for informed decision-making.

## ⚙️ Meeting Workflows


And it doesn’t stop there. The system handles reminders, rescheduling, and cancellations too.


**Reminders:** Automatic notifications keep prospects and sales teams on schedule, reducing no-shows.


**Rescheduling:** Quick updates ensure everyone stays aligned when plans change.


**Cancellations:** Tracks patterns and triggers follow-ups to re-engage prospects.
