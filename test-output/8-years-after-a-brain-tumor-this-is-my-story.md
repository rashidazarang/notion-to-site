# 8 Years After a Brain Tumor: This is My Story


![image](notion:f27d2a60-7c09-458b-95da-d56df35f6bd6)


Rashid Azarang recounts his life-altering experience beginning at age 18, when what seemed like an ordinary celebration of achievements was dramatically interrupted by a severe seizure and the discovery of a brain tumor. Amid the turmoil of hospital visits and the emotional toll of dealing with a serious illness at a young age, Rashid shares a deeply personal journey of introspection and self-discovery. He describes the existential questions that arose and how solitude and reflection led to newfound pleasures and a sense of inner peace even in the face of potential death. Rashid's story is one of resilience and transformation, detailing the profound impact of his medical ordeal and subsequent recovery. His narrative is a testament to the human spirit's ability to find strength and hope in the most challenging circumstances.
