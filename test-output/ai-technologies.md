# **AI Technologies**


### **Generative AI:**


I mainly utilize Claude and GPT-4 for robust content generation, crafting narratives and materials that cater to my unique needs.


### **Natural Language Understanding (NLU):**


Employing digitized voice recordings and custom Python scripts, alongside Apple Shortcuts, I create a mobile and user-centric experience, that facilitates accurate transcriptions and contextual understanding, enhancing my interactions and intellectual journey.


### **Sentiment Analysis:**


With AI-driven bots for data scraping and Airtable's REST API, I occasionally manage and interpret sentiment data, enabling me to leverage on customer sentiment trends and generate novel insights.


### **Personalized Recommendations:**


I implement algorithm/ that interact with my data to generate insights and recommendations that are both relevant and engaging.


### **Predictive Analytics:**


Algorithms are at the core of my predictive models, interacting with data to unveil insights that drive strategic decision-making and trend forecasting, essential for staying ahead in a dynamic market.


### **Computer Vision:**


Utilizing GPT Vision and Midjourney, I analyze visual content to support various operational needs, from real time analysis to design co-pilot.


### **AI Agents:**


I craft AI agents designed for specialized tasks, such as facilitating role-playing and simulations on predefined topics. These agents, equipped with specific formats and roles, serve to augment my thought processes and operational workflows. This tailored assistance not only enhances productivity but also flexibly adapts to meet my evolving requirements.
