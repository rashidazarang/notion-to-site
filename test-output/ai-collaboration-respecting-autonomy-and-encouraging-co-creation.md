# AI Collaboration: Respecting Autonomy and Encouraging Co-creation


I believe we should not scrutinize every response AI provides, rather, we should assign it a name and include it in the conversation as an autonomous entity. By acknowledging that we’re collaborating with AI, it simplifies our transparency process and eliminates the responsibility of identifying with everything AI articulates. In this way, we can preserve our own autonomy.


For instance, we can request that AI, when acting as an interpreter and catalyst for information, ask questions, and similarly, grant us space where we can, using our own voice and autonomy, add our comment. Thus, each party can collaboratively contribute to the argument or expression of an idea or text document.
