# Built a COVID-19 Testing Platform for 1M+ Users


📚 **Case Study**


🗓️ **2021-2022**


## Building **a COVID Testing Platform for LATAM**


In facing the unprecedented challenges of the global pandemic, Pronalab, a Spain-based laboratory, partnered with me to manage a staggering volume of COVID-19 tests. 


Discover how we scaled testing capabilities dramatically, from processing 1,000 tests per month to surpassing 100,000 tests per month.

## **Problem Statement**


Pronalab's testing centers were overwhelmed, with long lines from dawn to dusk. Operators and nurses faced the dual challenge of managing a high volume of manual processes and addressing unscheduled attendances. This not only stretched resources thin but also compromised user experience and operational efficiency. The situation called for an urgent need for streamlined processes and the introduction of automated, software-driven solutions to transform the existing system.


![image](notion:4d514fe3-891e-46ae-a096-db6e33ac3654)

## Objective


My primary objective was to revitalize Pronalab's testing operations through the introduction of innovative automation and software solutions. I aimed to significantly reduce the manual workload, enhance user experience for both staff and clients, and increase the overall efficiency of the testing processes. An additional goal was to ensure the scalability of operations to manage the high and fluctuating demand for COVID-19 tests, focusing on accuracy and timely delivery of results. This strategy was designed to address immediate challenges and set a foundation for sustainable operational excellence.

## **Design**


Next, I focused on the design aspect of the solution, which was critical to achieving the set objectives. This involved selecting the right tools and developing initial prototypes.


### **Tool Selection**


I carefully selected a toolkit comprising Airtable, Miniextensions, Zapier, Docupilot, Sendgrid, and Notion for their robustness, flexibility, and seamless integration capabilities. These tools were integral in automating and streamlining the processes at Pronalab.


### **Prototyping & Testing**


Before implementing the solution on a full scale, I developed prototypes to visualize and refine the digital transformation process. These prototypes were rigorously tested both internally and with select members of Pronalab's team to ensure their effectiveness and user-friendliness.


### **Security Compliance**


Given the sensitive nature of the data being handled, I established secure profiles for each operator and defined specific roles to maintain a protected workflow. Compliance with local privacy laws and regulations was a top priority throughout this phase.


---


## **Implementation**


With a clear roadmap in place, I began the digital transformation, ensuring each step was aligned with Pronalab's objectives.


### Operator Interface for Certificate Generation


As registrations accumulated, operators were prompted to verify the information and generate test certificates within a brief 1-2 minute window, ensuring prompt delivery of services.


![image](notion:46d587e4-2cef-4c70-9068-3d1a0ed8f342)


After changing the status to 'URL Created', emails with test results were dispatched to guests within a matter of minutes, showcasing the efficiency of the new digital system.


This streamlined process not only enhanced the efficiency of test result dissemination but also greatly improved the user experience by reducing wait times and administrative burdens on both staff and guests.


### **Streamlined Database Management**


Operators were equipped with a user-friendly interface on Airtable, complete with custom filters to track test creation, assignment, and results dissemination in real-time. The system allowed for quick sorting by test status, operator handling, and sending time, making the management of over a million tests not only possible but also efficient.


This interface was designed with intuitiveness in mind, ensuring that even operators with minimal technical experience could navigate the system with ease. Filtered views allowed for instant access to critical data points such as pending tests, completed tests, and tests awaiting results delivery.


![image](notion:d03334d2-4f14-407c-80c3-9ff9784971d9)


### **Efficient Transferability**


Moreover, the system's design facilitated easy transfer of tasks between team members. With a few taps, an operator could update test statuses, relay information to colleagues, or coordinate with hotel staff to ensure guests received their results promptly.


![image](notion:f53575ab-3e5d-49d1-b548-95c177948acd)


The flexibility of this system played a pivotal role in scaling operations and ensuring that no guest was left waiting for their test results, thereby significantly enhancing user satisfaction and trust in Pronalab's services.


The culmination of these efforts was a testament to the power of digital tools in transforming complex processes into streamlined, user-centric operations.
