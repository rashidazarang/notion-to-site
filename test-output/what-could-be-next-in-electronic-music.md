# My bet on what could be next in deep house.


I'm captivated by the idea of blending the profound wisdom of podcast talks, like those by Jordan Peterson, with the rhythm of deep house music. Enriching this fusion further with repetitive OM chants or empowering Hindu mantras can add a deeper, more mystical layer to the experience.


Such an innovative blend is not just audibly appealing; it also translates into an intensified mystical journey for the listener. It enables them to absorb the intellectual substance of the talks while being entranced by the music's hypnotic beats and the mantras' spiritual vibrations.


I had the pleasure of experiencing this captivating blend first-hand at Burning Man 2018, within the vibrant ambience of the Mayan Warriors Camp.


This video showcases the enchanting performance by Monolink, live on the Mayan Warrior boat at Playa during Burning Man 2018.


[**Monolink (live) - Mayan Warrior - Burning Man 2018**](https://youtu.be/IvwS6BYjHG0)

Pay close attention from the 3:33 mark onwards, and you will discern the underlying voice of Jordan Peterson, weaving his thought-provoking narratives into the music's soulful landscape.
