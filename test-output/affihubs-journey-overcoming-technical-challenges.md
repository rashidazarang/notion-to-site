# Overcoming Technical Challenges


When I embarked on the journey to create Affihub, it was with a clear vision: to revolutionize the way world-class talent offers services in diverse fields like entrepreneurship, tutoring, wellness, and mental health. This ambition was driven by the rapid shift to remote collaboration tools in the wake of COVID-19. I saw an opportunity to make a significant impact by connecting global talent in a way that was both universally accessible and empowering.


However, the path to realizing this vision was fraught with unexpected challenges. The most daunting of these was managing the complex Node.js codebase developed by an offshore team in India. My expertise lay in broader strategic and design aspects, not in the intricate details of computer science. Trusting in the team's technical capabilities, I focused on the overarching goals of Affihub, unaware of the issues lurking beneath the surface.


The realization hit hard when I discovered that the codebase was far from what Affihub required to succeed. This was a critical moment, one that forced me to confront my limitations and the consequences of relying heavily on an external team for such a vital element of the project. It was a harsh lesson in the importance of technical oversight and understanding.


Refusing to be deterred by this setback, I embarked on a self-driven quest to delve into the depths of computer science. This journey was both challenging and enlightening, reshaping my approach to project management and enriching my technical knowledge. It was a period of intense growth, driven by my commitment to the mission of Affihub.


Despite moments of doubt and frustration, especially when external reviews echoed the shortcomings of the codebase, I clung to my belief in Affihub’s mission. I persevered, navigating through the complexities and learning from each obstacle.


This challenging phase of my life turned into a transformative experience. I emerged not only with a deeper understanding of technology but also with a newfound appreciation for my capabilities. My vision for Affihub became even more resolute. I was determined to create a platform that not only facilitated the exchange of services but also embodied our core values of work-life balance and personal development.


In the end, this journey was about much more than rectifying a technical issue. It was about forging a path for a future where talent and skill could be shared freely across borders, creating a world where everyone can thrive by their rules, balancing demanding jobs and busy family lives. Affihub, in its essence, became a testament to this vision – a platform where passions are turned into valuable services, and where a community of people from all walks of life can come together to learn, grow, and achieve their fullest potential.
