**Evolving Go-To-Market Strategy**


# **From SaaS Tool to Trust-Based Marketplace**


![image](notion:11a52116-a8b1-80f3-abaf-e229f6025306)


### **1. Initial SaaS Tools for Event Organizers**


**Objective:** Provide event organizers, particularly local governments and cultural centers, with free tools to manage their events.


**Execution:** Offer a suite of tools that streamline event scheduling, booking, and management. Begin with key partnerships, such as municipal governments and cultural institutions, to establish an early user base.


**Outcome:** Affihub establishes itself as an indispensable tool for event management, offering a 10x improvement in efficiency for organizers.


---


### **2. Introduction of Creator and Community Pages**


**Objective:** Enhance user trust and transparency by showcasing the organizers and the communities behind each event or session.


**Execution:** Each event is tied to a public profile, whether that’s a creator or a cultural organization. These profiles not only host session details but also provide credibility, showing the user exactly who is behind the event.


**Outcome:** The integration of profiles creates an ecosystem where trust and accountability are enhanced, encouraging users to engage more confidently with the platform.


---


### **3. Expansion Towards a Directory of Events**


**Objective:** Transform Affihub into a go-to directory for discovering events and experiences in specific areas, starting with San Pedro.


**Execution:** Aggregate and centralize events across different sectors (cultural, educational, recreational) into one easy-to-navigate platform. Users can browse based on categories, locations, or interests.


**Outcome:** Affihub becomes synonymous with event discovery, giving users an extensive view of what’s happening around them while driving engagement and interaction.


---


### **4. Transition to Payment Integration**


**Objective:** Enable Affihub to capture value by facilitating transactions for workshops, multi-day sessions, and other paid events.


**Execution:** Integrate secure payment gateways, allowing creators and organizations to offer paid sessions directly through Affihub, starting with workshops and educational content.


**Outcome:** This adds a monetization layer for both users and creators, creating a marketplace dynamic and opening new revenue streams for the platform.


---


### **5. Evolving into a Marketplace**


**Objective:** Gradually evolve from a directory into a full marketplace where users not only discover, but interact, transact, and collaborate.


**Execution:** Start by facilitating connections between users and event organizers, and expand into areas like:

- **Peer-to-Peer Sessions:** Individuals offering personal tutoring, consulting, therapy, coaching, and specialized workshops.
- **Collaboration:** Enable creators to collaborate on events and share resources, developing joint sessions or multi-day events.

**Outcome:** Affihub becomes a versatile marketplace where users engage in a wide range of services and sessions, from personal consultations to collaborative workshops, fostering rich interactions and transactions.


---


### **6. Future Vision: A Marketplace of Collaborators and Creators**


**Objective:** Evolve into a fully interactive marketplace where creators can collaborate, transact, and build lasting connections.


**Execution:** Build out features that enable users to create joint projects, co-host events, and integrate social networking elements to strengthen collaboration.


**Outcome:** Affihub becomes a multifaceted platform where users not only discover events but engage in meaningful collaborations, driving continued growth and user retention.

### **What is Affihub?**


Affihub is a **platform that brings people together** by allowing them to share their passions, skills, and expertise with others in their community and beyond. We make it easy for individuals, professionals, and organizations to create and join events, workshops, and sessions—both online and in-person.


### **Our Unique Approach:**

1. **Community Integration:**
    - **Collaboration with Local Governments and Organizations:** We partner with municipalities, cultural centers, and community groups to enhance public events and activities.
    - **Support for Community Activities:** From running clubs to alumni associations, Affihub provides tools to organize and promote group activities effortlessly.
2. **Accessible and Affordable:**
    - **Free to Use:** There are no costs to join or create events on Affihub. We only charge a small 4% fee on successful transactions when you decide to monetize your sessions.
    - **Easy to Use:** Our platform is user-friendly, allowing anyone to set up an event or session in minutes.
3. **Curated Quality:**
    - **Careful Selection:** We curate the events and sessions offered to ensure high-quality experiences for all participants.
    - **Feedback and Improvement:** Users can provide feedback on sessions, helping us and creators continually improve.
4. **Genuine Human Connections:**
    - **Real-Time Interactions:** All sessions are live, fostering authentic connections between people.
    - **Collaboration Opportunities:** Creators can team up to offer joint sessions, enriching the experience for participants.
5. **Diverse Offerings:**
    - **Wide Range of Services:** Beyond events and workshops, professionals like psychologists and telemedicine providers offer their services through Affihub.
    - **Flexible Engagements:** Whether it’s one-on-one consultations, group workshops, or collaborative projects, Affihub accommodates various types of interactions.

### **How Affihub Benefits You:**

- **For Individuals:**
    - **Discover Local Events:** Find activities and sessions that match your interests in your area.
    - **Learn and Grow:** Access expertise from passionate individuals and professionals.
    - **Build Community:** Connect with others who share your interests.
- **For Creators and Professionals:**
    - **Share Your Passion:** Offer your skills and knowledge to those who value them.
    - **Grow Your Audience:** Increase your reach by appearing on our marketplace and community pages.
    - **Collaborate and Innovate:** Work with other creators to offer unique experiences.
- **For Organizations and Governments:**
    - **Enhance Community Engagement:** Promote events and activities without additional costs.
    - **Streamline Management:** Use our tools to organize events efficiently.
    - **Strengthen Community Bonds:** Foster a more connected and active community.

### **Why Choose Affihub Over Other Platforms?**

- **Community-Centric Focus:** Unlike generic marketplaces, Affihub is built around strengthening local communities and fostering genuine connections.
- **Partnerships with Public Entities:** Our collaboration with governments and organizations adds credibility and expands opportunities for users.
- **Affordable and Fair:** With minimal fees and a commitment to accessibility, we ensure that everyone can participate without financial barriers.
- **Quality Over Quantity:** Our curated approach means you get access to meaningful, high-quality experiences rather than an overwhelming array of options.

### **Getting Started with Affihub is Easy:**

1. **Sign Up for Free:** Create an account with just a few clicks.
2. **Explore or Create Events:** Browse available sessions or set up your own.
