# Who is Rashid


**Introducing**


## Who is Rashid?


**Building Cognitive Infrastructure at the Intersection of Data, AI, and Human Intent**


Rashid is a 34-year-old technology architect who transforms complex enterprise data into intelligent, accessible systems. With over a decade of experience spanning UX design, full-stack development, and AI integration, he specializes in building cognitive infrastructures that enable natural human interaction with structured data through Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and AI agents.


---


## Core Expertise


### **Data Architect & Integration Specialist**

- Designs and implements enterprise-scale data warehouse solutions for property management and insurance brokerage industries
- Creates custom analytics platforms that transform raw data into actionable business intelligence
- Builds ETL/ELT pipelines handling millions of records with real-time processing capabilities
- Specializes in API orchestration, connecting disparate systems into unified data ecosystems

### **Cognitive Infrastructure Pioneer**

- Develops MCP servers that bridge AI assistants with enterprise databases, enabling natural language data interactions
- Published the Airtable MCP Server (2024), integrating AI with business databases through Anthropic's Model Context Protocol
- Creates intelligent systems where AI agents can query, analyze, and act on centralized structured data
- Vision: Making enterprise data as conversational as talking to a colleague

### **Backend & Systems Developer**

- **Languages:** Python (MCP servers, data processing), TypeScript (enterprise systems), C#/.NET (backend services)
- **Data Platforms:** PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, Airtable API, Supabase
- **Integration Stack:** Model Context Protocol, REST/GraphQL APIs, webhook architectures
- **Cloud & DevOps:** Azure services, Docker containerization, CI/CD pipelines

### **AI & Automation Engineer**

- Builds AI agents that understand business context and execute complex data operations
- Developed systems serving 1M+ users, including COVID-19 testing platform across Latin America
- Creates automation frameworks that eliminate manual processes while maintaining data integrity
- Explores the frontier of AI-data interaction through projects like "Solving the Context Window Problem"

## Technical Philosophy


### **Cognitive Infrastructure Vision**


Rashid believes the future of enterprise software lies in cognitive infrastructure—intelligent layers that make complex data systems as intuitive as human conversation. His MCP servers and AI agents represent the first generation of tools that truly understand both data structure and human intent.


### **Data as a Living System**


Rather than treating databases as static repositories, Rashid designs data architectures that breathe, adapt, and respond intelligently to business needs. His systems don't just store information—they understand it, connect it, and make it actionable.


### **Human-Centered Technology**


Despite working with complex backend systems, Rashid maintains a UX designer's mindset. Every API endpoint, every data model, every integration is designed with the end user in mind—whether that user is a developer, analyst, or AI agent.
