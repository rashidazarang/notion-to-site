# **Going All In on Intelligence**


**Date:** August 18, 2025


People talk about “using AI” the way they talk about using a new app. You sign up, click around, maybe save some time. But that’s not really the point.


The real shift is learning how intelligence flows. Once you see that, the tools stop being toys. They become levers. And if you line them up right, you can compound what you know, how you decide, and how you build.


![image](notion:25352116-a8b1-80f5-b953-e7a65608ef5c)


That’s what I’m going all in on. Not just having AI draft an email, but structuring a system where agents handle tasks, coordinate with each other, and evolve alongside me. At first, I’ll micromanage. I’ll watch the gears turn. But the goal isn’t to get stuck there. The goal is to climb. To move from task manager, to system manager, to someone who only sets direction—while the structure itself compounds intelligence.


The endgame is strange but simple: to be able to step away entirely. To go tech-free, not because I’m rejecting it, but because I’ve built a foundation that carries forward without me. Independence through capability.


That’s why I’m all in.
