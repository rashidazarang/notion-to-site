# Supplementation Protocol


[← Go back](/c944ab370e904d82a4f9cecf32ff6dea)


## Supplementation Protocol


![image](notion:25952116-a8b1-80c4-a67a-d4fc00d17118)


### Goals

1. Maximize functional recovery within biological constraints
2. Prevent secondary neurodegeneration
3. Optimize neural tissue function
4. Manage ADHD while supporting brain repair

---


## Framework


### Pillar 1: Neuroprotection

- **Primary agents:** NAC (1200mg), Omega-3s (EPA 3000mg)
- **Supporting agents:** Theracurmin, Vitamin E (in omega-3s)
- **Mechanism:** Glutathione replenishment, anti-inflammatory cascades

### Pillar 2: Neuroplasticity Enhancement

- **Primary agents:** Lion's Mane (3000mg), CDP-Choline (1000mg), DHA (1000mg)
- **Supporting agents:** PQQ, NR+Resveratrol
- **Mechanism:** NGF/BDNF stimulation, membrane synthesis, mitochondrial biogenesis

### Pillar 3: Metabolic Support

- **Primary agents:** Creatine (5g), Ca-AKG (2000mg)
- **Supporting agents:** B-complex, CoQ10 (via PQQ pathway)
- **Mechanism:** ATP maintenance, Krebs cycle optimization

---


## Prioritization


### Tier 1: Strong Clinical Evidence

1. **NAC 1200mg** - Military TBI protocol, 86% symptom improvement
2. **Omega-3 (EPA 3000mg + DHA 1000mg)** - Multiple RCTs, 30% perfusion improvement
3. **Creatine 5g** - Pediatric TBI studies, significant cognitive gains
4. **CDP-Choline 1000mg** - Meta-analysis showing 18% functional independence increase

### Tier 2: Moderate Evidenced 

1. **Lion's Mane 3000mg** - Mori et al. RCT in MCI, NGF stimulation proven
2. **Phosphatidylserine 600mg** - Membrane repair, cortisol modulation
3. **Sulforaphane 35mg** - Animal TBI models, human autism/schizophrenia benefits
4. **Magnesium Glycinate 200mg** - NMDA modulation, sleep architecture

### Tier 3: Theoretical/Emerging

1. **Ca-AKG 2000mg** - Mitochondrial support, no direct TBI evidence
2. **PQQ 20mg** - Mitochondrial biogenesis, limited human data
3. **Spermidine 10mg** - Autophagy induction, one positive human trial
4. **NR + Resveratrol** - NAD+ support, general neuroprotection

---


## Timing


**Empty Stomach (optimal absorption):**

- NAC, PQQ, Sulforaphane, Ca-AKG
- 30-minute window before meals

**With Meals (fat-soluble/GI protection):**

- All fat-soluble vitamins (D3, K, E)
- Phospholipids (PS, CDP-Choline)
- Omega-3s (enhanced absorption with fats)

**Circadian Alignment:**

- Stimulating compounds (morning): PQQ, B-complex
- Neutral compounds (both): Most supplements
- Calming compounds (evening): Magnesium, Lithium, Melatonin

## Monitoring


### Biomarker Tracking


**Baseline (immediate):**

- CBC, CMP, Lipid panel
- Omega-3 Index
- Vitamin D levels
- Liver enzymes (AST, ALT, GGT)

**Monthly:**

- Liver function (high supplement load)
- Executive function assessment (MoCA or similar)

**Quarterly:**

- Full panel repeat
- Omega-3 Index (target >8%)
- Inflammatory markers (CRP, IL-6)

---


## Sustainability


### Year 1: Intensive Repair

- Full protocol as designed
- Monthly monitoring
- Quarterly adjustments based on response

### Year 2: Optimization

- Reduce experimental supplements if no clear benefit
- Maintain core interventions (NAC, Omega-3, Creatine, Lion's Mane)
- Consider prescription additions (Memantine)

### Lifetime Maintenance


**Forever required:**

- Omega-3s (EPA+DHA)
- Magnesium
- Basic antioxidant support
- ADHD medication

**Cyclical:**

- Lion's Mane (quarterly cycles)
- Intensive protocols (annually)

---


## Risk Management


### Safety Profile

- No dangerous drug interactions identified
- Bleeding risk from antiplatelet stack (monitor)
- Liver processing of 41 daily compounds (monitor enzymes)
- GI tolerance (split dosing helps)

### Failure Points Addressed

- Product quality verified
- Dosing corrected to therapeutic levels
- Timing optimized for absorption
- Compliance supported by organization system

---


## Rationale 


This protocol represents a systematic, evidence-based approach to maximizing functional c ognition. By addressing oxidative stress (NAC), inflammation (EPA), structural repair (DHA, PS), neuroplasticity (Lion's Mane), and metabolic support (Creatine, Ca-AKG), we create multiple synergistic pathways for compensation and adaptation.


The protocol prioritizes interventions with human clinical evidence, while cautiously including theoretical agents with strong mechanistic rationale and minimal risk. Cost-effectiveness has been optimized by eliminating ineffective products and ensuring therapeutic dosing of proven interventions.


Expected outcomes are realistic: 60-70% functional improvement represents the upper boundary of what's achievable with supplementation. However, for a 34-year-old with significant functional impairment, this degree of improvement could be life-changing.

## **Daily Schedule:**


### **Morning Routine:**


**Step 1 - Empty Stomach:**

- NAC - 1 capsule (600mg)
- PQQ - 1 capsule (20mg)
- BroccoMax - 1 capsule (17.5mg SGS)
- Ca-AKG - 2 capsules (1000mg)
- Creatine - 5g powder in water
- Inulin - 1 tsp powder (3.3g)

**Step 2 - With Breakfast:**

- Concerta - 54mg

- CDP-Choline - 3 capsules (750mg)
- Phosphatidylserine - 2 capsules (300mg)
- Omega-3 EPA - 3 softgels (1500mg EPA)

- Theracurmin - 1 capsule (300mg)
- Vitamin D3 - 1 softgel (2000 IU)
- Zeaxanthin/Lutein - 1 softgel
