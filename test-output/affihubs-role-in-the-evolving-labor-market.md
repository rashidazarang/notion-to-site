## **The Dual Effects of Technology on Employment**


**Destruction Effect:**


The advent of automation, artificial intelligence, and digital platforms has reignited fears regarding the displacement of jobs—a phenomenon Keynes warned about nearly a century ago. This "destruction effect" posits that technology-fueled disruption and automation could substitute capital for labor, leading to significant unemployment or the need for workers to transition to different sectors or roles. Historical precedents, such as the displacement of bookkeepers, cashiers, and telephone operators, illustrate this trend, highlighting the immediate, tangible impact of technological advancement on certain job categories. However, this effect is only one side of the coin.


**Capitalization Effect:**


Parallel to the destruction effect is the "capitalization effect," wherein technological innovation spurs the creation of new goods, services, occupations, and even industries. This effect underscores technology's role in generating demand for novel and enhanced forms of labor, thereby creating opportunities for employment growth in areas previously unimaginable. The app economy serves as a prime example of this dynamic, having emerged from the digital revolution to generate significant revenues and jobs within a relatively short span of time. Unlike the gradual transitions of past industrial revolutions, the current technological upheaval operates at a much faster pace, raising critical questions about the timing and extent of this capitalization effect.


Affihub situates itself as a crucial intermediary in this landscape, offering tools, resources, and a platform designed to bridge the gap between the destruction and capitalization effects of technology on employment. By facilitating access to continuous learning, skill development, and adaptation to emerging job markets, Affihub empowers individuals to navigate the complexities of this transition. The platform not only aims to mitigate the potential negative impacts of automation and digital disruption but also to capitalize on the new opportunities they present, ensuring that the workforce remains resilient, versatile, and prepared for the future.
