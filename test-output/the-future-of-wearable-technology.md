From humble beginnings of fitness trackers to the present-day wonders of smartwatches, wearable technology has been on a relentless journey of amplifying the human experience. Now, imagine a world where we integrate the sheer power of Artificial Intelligence (AI) with a microcontroller, sensor or microphone, and seamless connectivity options like WiFi and Bluetooth. It's not just a world of amplified potential; it's a reality that boosts the capability of wearables by an astonishing 10,000X!

## **Spotlight on Humane's Ai Pin**


![image](notion:2195f6e7-79fd-4178-a391-facb16a5e812)


Recent developments in the wearable domain have sent waves of excitement. Leading this pack is Humane's Ai Pin. Priced audaciously at $1,000, this device, reminiscent of the size of a saltine cracker, is more than what meets the eye. Housing features like a camera, microphone, speaker, and a laser projector, it is powered by the robust Snapdragon chip from Qualcomm.

Behind Humane's vision stand two formidable pillars: Imran Chaudhri and Bethany Bongiorno. With Imran's iconic contributions to consumer products like the Macintosh, iPod, and iPhone, and Bethany's pivotal role in the execution of monumental projects such as the original iPad, Humane stands as a testament to their combined brilliance. United by a vision, they're on a mission to redefine our tech future.


**Humane's Promise**:


At its core, Humane believes in the sanctity of the human experience. In an age of overwhelming technological advancements, Humane promises products that resonate with human touch, spark joy, and stem from genuine intentions. Their unwavering principle? Everyone deserves more from technology.


As November 9th looms closer, all eyes are on Humane's Ai Pin, a potential game-changer that might redefine how we perceive wearables. As technology continues to evolve, it's brands like Humane that push the envelope, ensuring we're not just part of the future, but actively crafting it.
