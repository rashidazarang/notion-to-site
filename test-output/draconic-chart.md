## 1. What is the Draconic Chart?


The Draconic Chart repositions all planetary placements based on your True North Node being placed at 0° Aries. This version of the birth chart reveals your soul’s essence, spiritual orientation, and pre-incarnational intentions.


**Key Insight**: Your Draconic chart is your soul’s blueprint, while your tropical natal chart is your personality’s interface. Together, they map the dance between soul and self.


## 2. Draconic Core Profile: Your Soul’s Blueprint


### Sun in Cancer – 10th House


Your soul is here to nurture through leadership. You’re meant to leave a meaningful legacy through emotional intelligence and care, not just achievement. Public life is a container for your inner depth.


### Moon in Pisces – 4th House


An emotionally empathic, highly intuitive soul. You are here to heal ancestral wounds, connect with your deep emotional body, and offer spiritual presence to others.


### Ascendant in Scorpio


You radiate a magnetic, transformative aura. Others feel your presence on a soul level—you’re not here to play it safe or surface-level. You embody the alchemist.


### Venus, Mars & Mercury in Gemini – 12th House


Your mind is tuned into invisible frequencies—ideas, symbols, dreams, and intuition. You are a quiet transmitter of insight, love, and curiosity from the soul realm. A natural writer, thinker, or spiritual messenger.


### Saturn, Neptune, Uranus in Pisces – 4th House (Retrograde)


This is heavy soul karma. You’ve chosen to transcend family pain, redefine spiritual structures, and embody mysticism with boundaries. Your wisdom may come from solitude, silence, or suffering transformed into light.


### Pluto in Aquarius – 2nd House (Retrograde)


Your soul seeks deep transformation around self-worth, value, and shared power. You are here to build ethical wealth and liberate your talents for the collective.
