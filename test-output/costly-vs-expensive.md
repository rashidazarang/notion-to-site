# **Costly vs. Expensive: What Really Matters**


![image](notion:adb27428-1e68-472c-93a6-d85b5bc79865)


Understanding the difference between “costly” and “expensive” transforms how we perceive value. “Costly” refers to a price that reflects intrinsic worth, craftsmanship, and durability. It implies attention to detail, quality materials, and longevity. For instance, a handmade chair may cost more because it’s built to last, designed with care, and crafted by skilled hands. While the upfront price is higher, the long-term reliability and aesthetic value make it a worthwhile investment.


In contrast, “expensive” signifies a high price tag without equivalent value. An expensive item may appear desirable due to branding or trends but often lacks the quality to justify its cost. For example, consider purchasing a flashy gadget that breaks within months. Despite the steep price, it fails to meet expectations, leaving you with frustration and regret. In such cases, the expense does not translate into genuine satisfaction or longevity.


Pierre-Alexis Dumas, Artistic Director at Hermès, illustrates this distinction by emphasizing the importance of craftsmanship over mere price. Hermès focuses on being costly—investing in quality, sustainability, and timeless design—rather than simply expensive for the sake of exclusivity. Their products become cherished items because they are functional, beautiful, and built to endure.


By evaluating whether something is costly or merely expensive, we make smarter decisions. Prioritizing lasting value over fleeting trends encourages us to choose products that enrich our lives and align with principles of sustainability. This mindset fosters an appreciation for quality and discourages wasteful consumerism driven by superficial appeal.


In a world of disposable goods and quick fixes, valuing cost over expense helps us invest in things that truly matter. The best purchases provide enduring benefits—functionality, satisfaction, and beauty—making them meaningful and worthwhile. By embracing this perspective, we support genuine craftsmanship and ensure that our choices reflect both practicality and purpose.
